Our Expertise

Domain of Research

Pushing the boundaries of material science through five multidisciplinary innovation tracks — from cryogenic engineering to biomedical breakthroughs.

01
01 Thermal Engineering

Cryogenic Insulation

Designed to minimize heat transfer at extremely low temperatures (below −150 °C). We develop ultra-low thermal conductivity aerogels and vacuum insulation panels to drastically reduce boil-off losses and improve energy efficiency in LNG storage and transport.

Below −150 °C Aerogels & VIPs LNG Storage Energy Efficiency
02
02 Protective Textiles

Extreme Weather Clothing

Engineering protection for the harshest environments on earth. We incorporate advanced aerogel insulation and phase change materials (PCMs) into multilayer textile systems to regulate heat transfer and maintain thermal comfort in polar and high-altitude conditions.

Aerogel + PCM Polar Environments High-Altitude Thermal Comfort
03
03 Biomedical Science

Biomedical Applications

Leveraging highly porous, biocompatible aerogel structures for drug delivery, tissue engineering, and wound healing. Our research focuses on controlled release mechanisms and scaffolding cell growth through tunable material properties and surface chemistry.

Drug Delivery Tissue Engineering Wound Healing Controlled Release
04
04 Defence & Tactical

Tactical Applications

Developing lightweight, multifunctional insulation for extreme defence environments. We augment protective gear with EMI shielding and infrared stealth capabilities, leveraging the exceptional high porosity and ultra-low density of aerogel composites for mission-critical performance.

EMI Shielding IR Stealth Ultralight Aerogel Protective Gear
05
05 Electronics & Energy

Electronic Insulation

Enabling next-generation thermal management and electrical insulation for advanced electronics. Our aerogel-based materials allow miniaturisation and improved safety in batteries, printed circuits, and high-performance computing devices operating at extreme thermal loads.

Thermal Management Batteries & PCBs HPC Devices Miniaturisation
